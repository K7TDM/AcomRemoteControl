Display & Sound

Raspberry Pi display

Connect a display to the Pi - the official touchscreen or an HDMI monitor - to show this dashboard on it, full screen, every time the Pi starts.

The screen dims to 5% after 10 minutes with no touch and no key-up; a touch or key-up brings it back.

Alarm sounds

In Morse, like the amp's own buzzer: W for a warning, S for a soft fault, a series of F for a hard fault. TEST when the amp powers up, R when it's ready, AR (.-.-.) when it powers off. Each device decides for itself. Browsers stay silent until the page has been tapped or clicked once.

Gauges

Show, size and reorder the dashboard's gauges (drag the ⠿ handle). Changes apply right away and are saved on the Pi, so every device shows the same layout.

    CAT / Rig Control

    Settings for the amp's own rear-panel CAT port (separate from this Pi's RS-232 link) - same options as the amp's own front- panel CAT menu. "Current" values come from the amp's own 0x12 reply, re-read each time Settings opens while the amp is connected.
